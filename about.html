About Joe Hood

I’m a Cambridge local with a lifelong passion for property and a career spanning lettings, sales, and property management. From managing branches to overseeing refurbishments, I’ve built a strong foundation in delivering results across every part of the sector.

Before property, I played professional football, an experience that shaped my discipline, work ethic, and drive for high performance. I now bring that same mindset into every transaction, ensuring clients receive a focused, proactive service from start to finish.

The property market is evolving quickly, and I believe success today requires a modern, forward-thinking approach. I tailor every strategy to the client’s goals, combining hands-on experience with market insight to maximise value and reduce risk.

I’ve worked across independent agencies and large corporate firms, and I understand the difference between standard service and truly client-focused results. My approach is personal, transparent, and built around communication at every stage.

Whether you're buying, selling, or letting, my goal is simple: deliver a seamless process and outstanding outcomes every time.
