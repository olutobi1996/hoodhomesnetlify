Specialist Lettings, Management & Property Strategy

Every property is different. At Hood Homes, we combine property management with practical, strategic advice to help owners decide how their property can best perform.

From traditional lettings and fully managed properties to short-term accommodation, HMOs, refurbishment and investment advice, we consider a range of options depending on the property and your objectives.

Property Lettings & Management

We take care of the entire lettings process, from marketing your property and securing suitable tenants through to managing the tenancy, rent collection, maintenance coordination, compliance and inspections. Designed for landlords who want their property professionally looked after while retaining clear visibility over how it is performing.

Short-Term & Flexible Letting Strategies

Traditional letting isn't the only option. Where appropriate, we assess whether a property may be better suited to short-term or serviced accommodation, managing the complete process including marketing, bookings, guest communication, preparation, cleaning coordination and maintenance.

Property Investment & Strategy

We help clients assess opportunities based on the property, location, likely costs, potential rental strategies and their longer-term objectives, including coordinating refurbishment and preparation before a property enters the rental market.

Guaranteed Rent & Property Partnerships

For selected properties, we can rent directly from the owner for an agreed period and take responsibility for its ongoing occupation and management, offering a more hands-off arrangement and greater certainty over rental income. Not every property is suitable, so we assess these opportunities individually.

Sales & Investment Advice

Although traditional estate agency sales are no longer our main focus, our experience within the Cambridge property market means we can still help when selling becomes the right strategy, advising on preparation, pricing and introducing trusted sales partners where required.

Compliance & Legislation

Rental legislation and compliance requirements continue to change. We stay up to date with regulatory requirements and market conditions, helping landlords make informed decisions while keeping their properties properly managed and compliant.

Managing Refurbishments

End-to-end refurbishment management including contractor coordination, budgeting, and project oversight to maximise property value.

Cambridge Coverage

Covering Cambridge City Centre, South Cambridge, North Cambridge, Eddington, Milton, Waterbeach, and surrounding villages.
