About Hood Homes

A modern approach to property, built on experience, discipline, and results.

Joe Hood

Founder, Hood Homes

I'm a Cambridge local with a lifelong passion for property and a career that spans every corner of the sector. From managing branches to overseeing refurbishments, lettings, property management, investment projects and sales, I've built a strong foundation of experience and a reputation for delivering results.

Today, Hood Homes is focused predominantly on lettings, property management and helping owners make the most of their property. Rather than taking a one-size-fits-all approach, we look at the property, its location, the market and the owner's objectives before recommending the strategy we believe is best suited to it — whether that's a traditional long-term tenancy, short-term or serviced accommodation, an HMO-style strategy, refurbishment and repositioning, or another approach altogether.

Our aim isn't simply to let a property. It's to understand what you own, what you want to achieve from it and how it can best perform in the current market.
